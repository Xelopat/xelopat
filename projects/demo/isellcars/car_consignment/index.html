How does the commission car sale process work?

Consultation and vehicle assessment.

At the first stage, we contact you to collect all required information: make and model, year, mileage, technical condition, trim level, and service history. Our experts assess the current condition of the car and perform a comparative market analysis of similar vehicles. This comprehensive assessment helps set a price that is attractive to buyers while remaining as beneficial as possible for you.

Technical diagnostics and vehicle preparation

After the price is agreed, the vehicle goes through a full technical inspection at our service area or partner stations. We detect and fix minor issues, and check suspension, engine, and electronics. If needed, we also perform cosmetic work: remove scratches, polish the body, clean the interior, and wash the car inside and out.

Professional photography and ad materials

A high-quality presentation is critical for a successful sale. Our photographers create a professional photo set from multiple angles: exterior, interior, technical details, and additional options. Based on these materials and technical specs, we prepare a clear, informative, and attention-grabbing listing. The copy highlights the key advantages of your car and answers likely buyer questions.

Listing placement and marketing promotion

The final listing is published on multiple trusted platforms, popular websites, social media, as well as on our own portal and in showrooms. We use targeted advertising and modern promotion methods to reach a broad audience and attract genuinely interested buyers. We track responses and adjust the strategy to deliver the best results.

Negotiations and viewing management

Our managers handle incoming buyer calls, answer all questions, provide additional details, and schedule meetings for vehicle viewings. We conduct negotiations professionally, protect your interests, and remove the communication burden from your side. If special conditions appear, our team resolves them quickly to reach a workable agreement.

Deal completion, handover, and payment

When a buyer is found, we take full legal support on ourselves: prepare and verify the sale contract, control the re-registration process, and organize key handover to the new owner. You receive payment in a safe and convenient format: cash or bank transfer. We guarantee transparency and no hidden risks at the final stage. After closing, you always get a full sales report and feedback.

The average sale period is from 2 to 6 weeks, but it depends on the brand, vehicle condition, and current demand.

What documents are required to transfer the car?

You need the original registration documents, sale agreement, diagnostic card (if required by law), and documents confirming technical condition.
